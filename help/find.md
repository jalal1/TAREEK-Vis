# Find a link, person or vehicle

1. Press Ctrl+F, or click the search box at the right of the ribbon tabs.
2. Type the MATSim ID of a link, a person or a vehicle.
3. Press Enter.

| You find | TAREEK-Vis does this |
|----------|----------------------|
| A link | Zooms to the link, shows it in yellow and shows its details on the Info page. |
| A vehicle | Shows a halo on the vehicle and shows the vehicle on the Info page. The map follows the vehicle while it drives. |
| A person | Shows a halo on the person and shows their day and route. The halo is on the vehicle while the person is aboard, and at the activity at other times. |

- The halo moves when the time changes.
- If a link, a person and a vehicle have the same ID, a list opens. Select
  the item that you want.
- In MATSim, a private car can have the same ID as its driver. TAREEK-Vis
  then shows one result: the person, in their vehicle.
- If nothing has the ID, a message shows below the search box.

To remove the halo, click an empty area of the map or click a different item.
