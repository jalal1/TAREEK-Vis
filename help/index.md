# TAREEK-Vis Help

TAREEK-Vis shows the output of a MATSim simulation on a map. You can play
the vehicles over the day, examine roads, vehicles and persons, compare the
simulation with traffic counts, and export maps, charts and videos.

Press **F1** in TAREEK-Vis to open the help topic for the ribbon tab that is
open.
