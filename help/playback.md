# Play the simulation

The timeline at the bottom of the window shows the simulation time as
hh:mm:ss from midnight. A MATSim day can continue after 24:00:00.

| To | Do this |
|----|---------|
| Play or pause | Click **Home > Play** or press Space. |
| Go to the start | Click **Home > Go to Start** or press Home. |
| Go to the end | Click **Home > Go to End** or press End. |
| Go to a time | Drag the timeline slider. |
| Change the speed | Click **Home > Speed**, then select a speed. |

- The speeds are 0.5x to 100x. At 10x, 10 simulation seconds go by in 1
  second.
- While you drag the slider, playback pauses. It continues when you release
  the slider.
- Playback stops at the end of the simulation.
- The status bar shows the number of vehicles that are on the network at the
  current time.
