# Public transit

The transit layers need a transit schedule file in the run folder. See
[Open a simulation run](open-a-run.md).

## Show routes and stops

Use the **Transit** tab. All transit layers are off at the start.

| Button | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| Bus, Tram, Rail | The routes of that mode. |
| Bus Stops | The stops. |
| Route Thickness | The line width of the routes: Thin (1 px), Medium (2 px) or Thick (4 px). |

All bus routes have one color. Each tram route and each rail route has its
own color.

## Examine a stop

Click a stop. The **Info** page shows the stop name, the stop ID, its
location, the lines that serve the stop and its network link. Click a line
to see that line.

## Examine a route

Click a route on the map. The route has a highlight. The **Info** page shows:

- The line.
- The stops of the route, in order. Click a stop to see that stop.
- The number of departures, and the first and last departure.

To remove the highlight, click an empty area of the map.

## Transit vehicles

To show or hide buses, trams and trains, use **View > Vehicles**. Click a
transit vehicle to see its line and its passengers. See
[Vehicles and persons](vehicles-and-persons.md).
